Subscriptions & Refunds

Effective date: 14 November 2026

CookingBear Plus is an optional subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds. We never see or store your payment details.

1. Plans

  • Yearly – billed once a year. New subscribers may get a 7-day free trial.
  • Monthly – billed once a month.

The current price in your currency is shown in the app before you confirm the purchase. Prices may vary by country and may change; Apple will tell you before an existing subscription renews at a higher price where required.

2. Free trial

  • If a free trial is offered, you will not be charged during the 7 days.
  • Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts automatically and you will be charged the yearly price.
  • A free trial is available once per Apple Account. Any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.

3. Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, and your Apple Account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends, unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

4. How to cancel

On your iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose CookingBear Plus, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also do this from Profile > Manage subscription in the app (when you are a member).

After cancelling, you keep Plus until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all my data", does not cancel your subscription.

5. Refunds

Because purchases are made through Apple, we cannot issue refunds ourselves. To request one:

  1. Go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Tap I'd like to, choose Request a refund, pick a reason and follow the steps.
  3. Choose the CookingBear Plus purchase and submit.

Apple decides refund requests under its own policies (https://support.apple.com/118223). Your legal rights always apply on top of that:

  • UK, Ireland and the EU: you have a 14-day right to cancel digital purchases. If you ask for Plus to start straight away, that right may end once it has started, as Apple tells you at purchase. A faulty service can still be repaired, replaced or refunded.
  • Australia and New Zealand: you are entitled to a remedy if the service has a major problem; this cannot be excluded.
  • United States, Canada and elsewhere: you have the rights given by your local consumer laws, including automatic-renewal and cancellation laws.

Apple handles all of these requests, because Apple takes the payment.

6. Price changes and reminders

If the price of your subscription goes up, Apple tells you in advance and, where the law requires, asks you to agree before you are charged the new price. Apple emails you a receipt for every renewal, and you can see your renewal date at any time in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

7. Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or use a new device with the same Apple Account, tap Restore purchases in the app to get your subscription back. You don't need to pay again.
