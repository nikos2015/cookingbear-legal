Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

CookingBear ("the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. CookingBear has no servers, no user accounts on our side, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We never receive your recipes, preferences, photos, voice or any other information, and we cannot see how you use the app.

2. What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your iPhone:

  • Preferences – your onboarding answers (allergies, spice level, time budget, cuisines), nutrition goal and voice settings.
  • Favourites and cook history – recipes you saved and how many times you finished cooking each one.
  • Meal plans and progress – your weekly meal plan and shopping-list ticks, the dates you finished cooking (for your weekly streak and badges), and the outfit you picked for the bear.
  • Usage counters – how many free uses of CookingBear Plus features you have left, and whether you have already seen certain tips or prompts.
  • Recipes you teach the bear – recipes you import from a link or a photo, together with their photos.
  • No account – CookingBear does not need or offer an account. You never sign in, and we never learn your name or email address.

This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device, which are controlled by you and Apple. You can delete all of it at any time — see section 6.

3. Features that use your device's hardware

All of these run on your device. Nothing is uploaded by us.

  • Camera and photos – used only when you choose to scan your fridge or counter for recipe ideas, or to photograph a recipe for the bear to learn. Images are analysed on-device with Apple's Vision framework. Recipe photos you keep are saved on your device as described above; fridge-scan images are not kept.
  • Microphone and speech recognition – used only when you ask the app to listen: while Hands-Free Cooking Mode is switched on, to recognise short voice commands like "Next" and "Repeat", and when you tap Ask me to ask Chef Bear a cooking question out loud. Recognition runs on your device with Apple's Speech framework when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech service may process the audio to recognise what you said (Apple's privacy policy applies). We never receive, record or store the audio.
  • Location (approximate) – used only when you tap Find Restaurants and allow it. Your iPhone gives the app a rough position (about 1 km), which is used once to search Apple Maps for places near you, then discarded. It is never stored, and never sent to us or anyone else. You can say no and type a town or postcode instead; the last one you typed is remembered on your iPhone only, so you can search it again in one tap.
  • On-device intelligence – where available, the app uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models to turn imported text into a structured recipe, to answer the cooking questions you ask Chef Bear and to make Twists (new versions of a recipe) ("Ask the Bear"). This processing happens on your device; your questions are not sent to us or stored.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in Settings > CookingBear.

4. Network connections

The app only connects to the internet in three cases:

  • Teach the Bear (recipe import from a link) – when you paste a web address, the app downloads that page and its recipe image directly from the website you chose, so it can read the recipe. That website will see a normal request from your device (such as your IP address), as with any web browser, and its own privacy policy applies. We do not receive or log this.
  • Find Restaurants – the app sends your search (the dish or cuisine, plus your approximate area or the town you typed) to Apple Maps to find places near you. Apple's privacy policy applies. We do not receive or log this, and no restaurant results are saved.
  • Purchases – subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes your payment and tells the app whether you have an active subscription. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal data to anyone, because we do not have any.

6. Deleting your data

Open Profile > Legal & privacy > Delete all my data. This permanently deletes, from your device: all preferences, favourites, cook history, usage counters and prompt state; every recipe and photo you taught the bear; and your meal plans. Deleting the app also removes this data (except any copy in your own device backups).

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your device.

7. Children

CookingBear is a general-audience cooking app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us using the details below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side; all app data is on your device and under your control as described in section 6. You may still contact us with any question.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes.
